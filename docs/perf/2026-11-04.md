# Performance, 2026-11-04

Measured on the owner's TV: Android 11 (API 30), 32-bit ARM, 1990 MiB of RAM, 1920x1080, which
`DeviceTier` calls MID. Method: `scripts/perf.sh`, one fixed walk of 12 key presses at 0.45 s.

| | Before, debug | After, debug | After, perf: runs 1 and 2 | Forced LOW, perf |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frames | 518 | 302 | 308, 331 | 327 |
| Deadline missed | 36 (6.9%) | 7 (2.3%) | 3 (1.0%), 9 (2.7%) | 4 (1.2%) |
| 50th percentile | 18 ms | 7 ms | 6 ms, 6 ms | 5 ms |
| 95th percentile | 36 ms | 21 ms | 17 ms, 20 ms | 18 ms |
| 99th percentile | 53 ms | 44 ms | 27 ms, 48 ms | 34 ms |
| Native heap | 18.6 MB | 13.5 MB | 11.2 MB, 13.8 MB | 12.5 MB |
| Graphics | 31.3 MB | 12.7 MB | 12.9 MB, 15.1 MB | 14.9 MB |
| Total PSS | 63.7 MB | 42.4 MB | 32.3 MB, 36.5 MB | 35.0 MB |

The "before" walk had 13 keys rather than 12, so compare rates and percentiles, not counts.
Runs are few and differ visibly: treat these as a clear direction, not exact gains. A
"janky" figure is left out on purpose: it counts input latency from `adb shell input`.

## What PSS does not show

- **Window buffers:** three at 8.1 MB each, 24.3 MB, held on behalf of the app in every tier
  (`dumpsys SurfaceFlinger`). `Window.setFormat(RGB_565)` does not change them on API 30, so it was
  removed.
- **The MID wallpaper:** about 8 MB of `Bitmap.Config.HARDWARE`, absent from these figures, which
  is why LOW reads no lower than MID here. LOW really does hold about 1 MB of wallpaper instead.

Add both when reasoning about a whole device.

## Not measured

No 1 GB TV has been available. LOW was forced on a 2 GB TV, which shows its behaviour and its
memory, not how a slower chip with less free RAM copes. The cold start time and a grid of 60+
apps have not been measured either.

## What made the difference

- A second home screen stayed alive per task (HOME and a launcher tile): 168 MB in the first reading.
- `clipPath` per card and per pane became `drawRoundRect` with a shader.
- Offscreen buffers from fading overlapping views: `hasOverlappingRendering` is false where it can be.
- Elevation at rest on every card removed.
- The lean, parallax and sheen, which redrew cards every frame, removed.
- Glass baked with its veil and sheen, one textured quad instead of four full-area blends.
- The wallpaper is drawn only while visible, on the GPU only, decoded and baked off the main thread.
- The clock stopped ticking while hidden, and ticks on the minute instead of every 15 s.
